# Seungmin Son · 손승민

**M.S. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science**  
Microwave Sensing and Imaging Laboratory · Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)  
Advisor: Prof. Kangwook Kim

I study how antennas shape electromagnetic fields. One question guides my current work: **how much of a plane wave can an antenna array reproduce at a finite distance?** I look at the phase across an observation plane and use that map to study the quiet zone of a near-field plane-wave generator.

### At the workbench

| | What I work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Antennas** | Compare conventional and resistively loaded dipoles, and estimate their phase centers from simulated fields. |
| **Numerical electromagnetics** | Write 1D, 2D, and 3D finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) solvers in MATLAB. Use CST field exports to synthesize arrays and study how phase varies with distance. |
| **RF measurement** | Develop MATLAB controls for a vector network analyzer and a robot arm to scan antenna fields point by point. |

### From radar to radiation

My undergraduate work at Hannam University used bistatic radar and range–Doppler maps to distinguish birds from drones. At GIST, my focus has shifted from the received echo to the field produced by the antenna. I’m also interested in Sommerfeld’s treatment of waves near an interface and its connection to surface-wave propagation.

I keep my derivations and physical interpretations in Korean. [Read my notes on electromagnetics ↗](https://sonzigusta.github.io/notes.html)
